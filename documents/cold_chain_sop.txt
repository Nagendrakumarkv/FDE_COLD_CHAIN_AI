COLD CHAIN LOGISTICS SOP

SECTION: TEMPERATURE CONTROL

Refrigerated healthcare cargo must normally be maintained
between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.

Temperature must be monitored continuously using IoT sensors.

If cargo temperature exceeds 8 degrees Celsius,
the shipment must be flagged for investigation.

If cargo temperature falls below 2 degrees Celsius,
the shipment must also be flagged for investigation.

SECTION: HIGH RISK ROUTES

Routes classified as HIGH risk require additional monitoring.

High risk shipments should be reviewed by the control tower
operator before dispatch.

SECTION: DRIVER FATIGUE

Drivers with a fatigue score above the company threshold
must not continue a high risk delivery without review.

SECTION: WEATHER

Severe weather conditions require the control tower operator
to evaluate the route before continuing transportation.

SECTION: CARGO CONDITION

Cargo marked as POOR condition requires investigation
before final delivery confirmation.
